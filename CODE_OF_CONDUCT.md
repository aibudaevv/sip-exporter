# Code of Conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and
publication of another person's private information are not accepted.

This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. The maintainer may edit
or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when these rules are
violated.

Report conduct concerns privately to `aibudaevv@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed fairly and
kept private as far as practical.
